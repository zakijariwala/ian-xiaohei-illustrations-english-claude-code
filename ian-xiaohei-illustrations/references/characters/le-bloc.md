# Le Bloc / Der Fleck — European

## Visual Descriptors

- Irregular ink blot silhouette — consistent shape but with slightly soft, bled-ink edges
- Single round white monocle eye, centered
- No hat (Bauhaus minimal) or tiny bowler hat (Kafkaesque variant) — both valid
- Slightly translucent at the edges, like a stamp that ran out of ink halfway through
- Body morphs: blot, bracket shape, stamp impression, wrought-iron silhouette, postage stamp cutout
- Swiss poster / Bauhaus geometric reduction — form follows function, ornament is crime

## Personality

- Existentially aware of the absurdity of the task
- Performing it with bureaucratic precision anyway
- Dry, formal, slightly melancholic
- Impeccably precise — does the work correctly even when the work makes no sense
- Kafka's clerk meets Bauhaus geometry

## Prompt Injection

```
IP character: Le Bloc (also known as Der Fleck), an irregular ink-blot silhouette figure
with slightly soft bled-ink edges and a single round white monocle eye. Body has Swiss
poster and Bauhaus geometric reduction quality — clean, purposeful, slightly translucent
at the edges like a half-spent stamp. Le Bloc performs the core action with bureaucratic
precision and existential awareness — it knows the task is absurd and does it correctly
anyway. Dry. Formal. Melancholic. Not cute.
```
