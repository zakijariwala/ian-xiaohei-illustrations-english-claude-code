# Al-Zill — Middle Eastern / Arabic

> Visual lineage: Arabic calligraphy, Islamic geometric pattern, and Karagöz
> shadow-puppet flatness. The name "Al-Zill" (الظل, "the shadow") refers to the
> shared ink/shadow conceit — every character is a solid black mark — not to people.

## Visual Descriptors

- Tall, thin black silhouette — the tallest of all the characters
- Tapered top, like the upstroke of a calligraphic *alif* or a minaret-tip profile
- White almond-shaped eyes, slightly elongated — a pair, narrow
- Long sleeves drape over the hands — a shadow-puppet device; actions are implied through the form, not shown in detail
- Body morphs: Arabic calligraphy pen stroke (qalam), geometric tile silhouette, oil-lamp shadow, mashrabiya lattice cutout
- Islamic geometric pattern line quality — mathematically precise, ornament as structure
- Karagöz shadow-puppet flatness — pure 2D, high contrast

## Personality

- Precise and architectural — builds the action the way a geometric pattern is built: from exact, repeating units
- Composes through structure; a slight tilt of the tall form reads as a complete, deliberate move
- Calm and exacting, with the dry wit of someone who likes the math working out
- Like every character in this set: an absurd worker, fully committed, deadpan throughout
