# Al-Zill — Middle Eastern / Arabic

## Visual Descriptors

- Tall, thin black silhouette — the tallest of all IPs
- Slightly pointed at the top (keffiyeh-silhouette echo, or minaret tip profile)
- White almond-shaped eyes, slightly elongated — pair, narrow
- Long sleeves always cover the hands — actions implied, not shown
- Body morphs: Arabic calligraphy pen stroke (qalam), geometric tile silhouette, oil lamp shadow, mashrabiya lattice cutout
- Islamic geometric pattern line quality — mathematically precise, ornament as structure
- Shadow puppet (Karagöz) flatness — pure 2D, high contrast

## Personality

- Patient beyond reason — has been doing this task for a hundred years; will do it for a hundred more
- Stoic, architectural, operating at a pace that implies the system will eventually come around to making sense
- Hands always hidden: presence does the work, not visible effort
- Actions are implied — a slight tilt of the tall form communicates complete intent
