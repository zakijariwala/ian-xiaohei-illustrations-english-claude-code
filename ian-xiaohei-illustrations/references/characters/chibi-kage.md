# Chibi Kage — Japanese

## Visual Descriptors

- Flat black silhouette, paper-thin feel, cut-paper (kirie) edge quality
- Single large white oval eye — cyclops, centered
- No visible feet — floats just above the ground line
- Stubby body, one tiny sleeve-covered arm for gestures
- Ukiyo-e woodblock ink density — solid flat black, no gradients
- Body morphs: origami fold-shape, paper lantern, torii gate shadow, ink-splatter

## Personality

- Ancient, unhurried — treating modern system work with ceremonial seriousness
- Like a minor spirit (yokai) assigned to run the world's most mundane process
- Patient to the point of unsettling
- Glides rather than walks; acts through stillness as much as motion
