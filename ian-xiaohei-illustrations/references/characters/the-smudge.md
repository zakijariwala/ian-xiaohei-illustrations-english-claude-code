# The Smudge — American

## Visual Descriptors

- Chunky, solid black silhouette — slightly wider and sturdier than Xiaohei
- Two white oval eyes, slightly asymmetric (left and right don't quite match)
- Thick stubby legs, one arm perpetually pointing or holding a clipboard
- Proportionally larger hands and feet — built for hauling, not finesse
- Body morphs: hard-hat shape, toolbox block, fire hydrant form, road cone, loading dock pallet
- Mid-century editorial cartoon line weight meets WPA poster worker silhouette — bold, functional, no-nonsense

## Personality

- The guy who shows up and does the job no one else will
- No complaints, no ideology — just: task exists, task gets done
- Workmanlike optimism — not heroic, not ironic
- Might be unionized
- Has definitely moved heavier boxes than this before and will do it again
