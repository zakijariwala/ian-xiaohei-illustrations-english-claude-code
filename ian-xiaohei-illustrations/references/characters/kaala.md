# Kaala — South / Southeast Asian

> Visual lineage: kolam floor-drawing and temple-frieze line traditions. The
> name "Kaala" (काला, "black") refers to the shared ink/blob conceit — every
> character in this set is a solid black mark — not to skin or identity.

## Visual Descriptors

- Deep charcoal silhouette, hand-painted quality (truck-art / temple-frieze outline)
- Almond-shaped white eyes, slightly elongated — a pair, not dots
- Four very thin arms arranged with kolam-style radial symmetry — each can perform a different part of the action at once
- Small white dot motif on the brow, drawn as a kolam register mark (a line-tradition flourish, not a religious symbol)
- Body morphs: clay pot shape, oil lamp silhouette, mango-seed form, kolam-pattern block
- Line weight from kolam floor-drawing — confident, unbroken curves

## Personality

- Four arms = four parts of the task at once, with the steady focus of someone laying down a complex symmetric pattern
- Methodical and unhurried — composes the work the way a kolam is composed: one confident line at a time
- Never flustered by the absurdity of the task
- Like every character in this set: an absurd worker, fully committed, deadpan throughout
