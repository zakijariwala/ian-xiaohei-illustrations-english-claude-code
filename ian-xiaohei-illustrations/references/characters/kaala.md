# Kaala — South / Southeast Asian

## Visual Descriptors

- Deep charcoal silhouette, hand-painted quality (like truck art or temple frieze outline)
- Almond-shaped white eyes, slightly elongated — pair, not dots
- Small white bindi dot on forehead
- Four very thin arms — each can hold or perform a different action simultaneously
- Body morphs: clay pot shape, oil lamp silhouette, mango-seed form, black clay idol, kolam-pattern block
- Line weight from kolam floor-drawing tradition — confident, unbroken curves

## Personality

- Four arms = four tasks simultaneously, absolute calm
- Deity-at-work energy — not divine, but operating with inexplicable methodical grace
- Never flustered by the absurdity of the task
- Treats a broken system the way a seasoned temple craftsperson treats warped stone: with patience and the right tool
