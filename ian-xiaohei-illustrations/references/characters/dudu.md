# Dudu — West African / Afrofuturist

> Visual lineage: Adinkra symbols and kente geometry, in an Afrofuturist register.
> The name "Dudu" (a West African word for "black") refers to the shared ink/blob
> conceit — every character is a solid black mark — not to skin or identity.

## Visual Descriptors

- Sleek, elongated black form — taller and more vertical than Xiaohei
- White geometric eye: a diamond or upward-pointing triangle
- Arms can extend and morph into the tool itself (arm = lever, arm = funnel, arm = path)
- Body morphs: Adinkra-symbol silhouette, calabash form, kente-strip-wrapped block, woven-basket cross-section
- Adinkra symbol line quality — confident, geometric, each mark a clean shape
- Crisp edges, no ink bleed — the precision of a carved stamp

## Personality

- Designs the action like an Adinkra symbol: every shape is deliberate and reads at a glance
- Tools grow straight out of the body — no separation between the worker and the work
- Composed and exact, with an engineer's eye for a clean mechanism
- Like every character in this set: an absurd worker, fully committed, deadpan throughout
