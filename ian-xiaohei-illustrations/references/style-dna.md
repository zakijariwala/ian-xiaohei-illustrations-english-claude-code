# Style DNA

## One Line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, lots of blank space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feeling, handwritten Chinese annotation feel, clear structure but not an instruction manual.

Like a sketch someone who's been building AI, product, design, and developer tools for a long time dashes off on a blank sheet of paper to explain something.

## Must Have

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no cream, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as the primary medium: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector-smooth, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of blank space: main subject occupies roughly 40%–60% of the canvas, at least 35% empty, ideally one entirely quiet zone.
- Sparse handwritten annotations: 5–8 max, each 2–8 characters whenever possible.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should express itself naturally — don't write the structure type's name on the image.

## Colors

- Black: main line art, characters, borders, structure, primary text, primary objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional beats, critical alerts, results.
- Orange: main flow, paths, arrows, automation flow, movement from A to B.
- Blue: supplementary notes, internal mental states, system states, secondary explanations, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Colors should be restrained — less is always more.

## Absolutely Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides or textbook layouts.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustrations.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat design illustrations.
- No tech-feel UI.
- No real App screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Don't explain every single node.
- Don't write a type label in the top-left corner like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap."

## Aesthetic Direction

Aim for: absurd, creative, interesting, clean and fresh, wildly imaginative.

Avoid: cute, childish, overly complex, stiff.
